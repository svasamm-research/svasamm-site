Run your entire healthcare facility on one platform - appointments, records, billing, lab, pharmacy, and HR. Built India-first, live in as little as 14 days.

Lucoze is Svasamm's India-first Hospital & Clinic Management System (HMS/EMR). It runs an entire facility — appointments, patient records, billing, lab, pharmacy, and HR — on one platform, built around Indian workflows and compliance (ABDM/ABHA, DPDP 2023, GST e-invoicing) with all patient data hosted in India and a Hindi-first interface. Clinics and small hospitals go live in as little as 14 days, with no data lock-in.

  • One platform, every department — appointments, EMR, billing, lab, pharmacy, and HR on a single patient timeline and ledger
  • ABDM-ready — ABHA-linked records with HPR/HFR and consent-manager integration
  • India data residency & DPDP 2023 — every byte in Indian data centres, consent and breach flows built in
  • GST-compliant billing — CGST/SGST/IGST invoicing and e-invoicing, pharmacy and OPD on one ledger
  • Lab & pharmacy done right — barcoded sample-to-report chain of custody; FEFO expiry-first dispensing with Schedule-H registers
  • Live in 14 days, Hindi-first UI — fast implementation, no six-month rollout, no proprietary lock-in

Clinics (1–15 doctors) Hospitals (20–100 beds) Diagnostic Centers / Labs Pharmacies Polyclinics Multi-specialty Practices
