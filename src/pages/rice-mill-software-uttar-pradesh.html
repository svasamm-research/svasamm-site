How paddy procurement works in Uttar Pradesh

UP paddy procurement is run by the state Food & Civil Supplies Department through its portal, fcs.up.gov.in. Farmers register online, and purchase happens at centres using biometric E-PoP (Electronic Point of Purchase) devices, with payments routed through PFMS; the Kharif procurement season typically opens around 1 October. Paddy procured at MSP is then custom-milled by empanelled rice mills, which deliver rice back to the central pool — the Custom Milled Rice (CMR) system. What Uttar Pradesh millers need from software

  • CMR compliance — track allotment vs delivery, the staged delivery schedule, the 67% + FRK obligation, and the out-turn (7.1) reconciliation.
  • Milling recovery — measure real recovery against the assumed out-turn so shortfalls don't quietly eat margin (see the recovery guide).
  • By-product accounting — bran, husk, and broken booked as separate revenue (see by-products).
  • Quality grading & moisture deductions handled correctly at the gate.
  • Hindi-friendly screens so mill staff work in their own language.
  • GST billing for the open-market side of the business.
