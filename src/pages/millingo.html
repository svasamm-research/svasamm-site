Millingo - ERP Built for Rice Mills

Not a generic ERP bent to fit - Millingo runs the whole mill: paddy procurement, quality grading, milling yield and by-products, packaging, and full government FCI/levy compliance.

Millingo is Svasamm's ERP built specifically for rice mills. It runs the whole mill end to end — paddy procurement and quality grading, milling production with yield and by-product tracking, packaging and gunny-bag accounting, market sales, and full government FCI/state levy (CMR) procurement and compliance. Offered in Starter, Professional, and Enterprise tiers, it scales from a single seasonal mill to multi-unit government-supplying operations.

  • Paddy procurement, gate-to-godown — purchase agreements (Sauda), gate receipts, and stock that only posts after quality approval
  • Quality grading with automatic butta deductions — multi-parameter QC (moisture, foreign matter, recovery) that deducts value from the supplier bill without touching quantity
  • Milling yield & by-product tracking — every batch tracks rice recovery % plus bran, husk, and broken rice, with by-product profitability
  • Gunny-bag & packaging (bardana) accounting — bag-type conversions (jute/PP), packing, and empty-bag returns
  • Government FCI / state levy procurement & CMR — levy memos, field camp-pick with GPS geofencing, CMR deliveries, release orders, and 7.1 out-turn reconciliation
  • Transport & weighbridge tracking — lorry and freight handling with kata-slip capture for inbound paddy
  • Rice-mill roles, dashboards & mobile field app — owner, purchase, QC, production, and warehouse queues, plus a phone-based camp-pick app

Frequently asked questions

What is a rice mill ERP?

A rice mill ERP is software built specifically for rice milling — paddy procurement and quality grading, milling production with yield and by-product tracking, packaging, sales, accounting, and government FCI/levy (CMR) compliance — on one system, rather than a generic ERP adapted to fit.

Does Millingo handle government CMR / levy procurement?

Yes. Millingo covers the full CMR cycle — levy memos, camp pickups, CMR deliveries and release orders, and the 7.1 out-turn reconciliation — with state-specific workflow (e.g. West Bengal e-Paddy). See the CMR guide.

Can Millingo track milling recovery and by-products?

Yes. Each milling batch computes rice recovery and books bran, husk, and broken rice as separate stock with their own valuation, so you see true profit per batch. See the recovery guide.

Which tiers does Millingo offer?

Starter (small/seasonal, market-only mills), Professional (full operations, financials, quality/moisture slabs, by-product P&L), and Enterprise (government CMR business, statutory returns, multi-unit).
