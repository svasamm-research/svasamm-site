Distributor Management System

DMS - Distributor Management System

Run your entire independent-distributor network on one platform. Built for OEM manufacturers who sell through distributors - configurable to your products, not custom-coded.

Svasamm DMS gives OEM manufacturers a single platform to run their distribution network end to end. Each distributor works in a scoped portal and sees only their own orders, invoices, deliveries, and stock, while you get a consolidated view across the entire network. New products, territories, and even new clients are onboarded by configuration — not code.

  • Distributor-scoped portals — each distributor sees only their own sales orders, invoices, delivery notes, and stock (enforced at the data layer)
  • OEM-to-distributor stock push — bulk stock allocation to distributor warehouses with serial tracking, movement reports, and low-stock alerts
  • Sales cycle & commissions — sales order to invoice to delivery, with configurable slab-based commission calculated automatically
  • Territory control — pincode-to-territory mapping with automatic cross-territory and off-territory sale flagging
  • Installed base & field service — equipment models, default consumable packages, and a field-service engineer hierarchy
  • CRM & helpdesk, distributor-scoped — leads, deals, and field visits per distributor, with role-based dashboards
  • Configure, don't code — product categories, consumables, commission slabs, and branding are settings; bulk-import your existing masters to go live fast
