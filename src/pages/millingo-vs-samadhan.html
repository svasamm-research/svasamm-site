Millingo vs Samadhan Rice Mill ERP

By Mithun K. Singh, Founder, Svasamm Research · 14 July 2026

Samadhan Rice Mill ERP is another established option for Indian mills, so it's a fair one to weigh against Millingo. Both target rice milling, but they're built on different foundations and lead with different strengths. Here's an honest side-by-side.

Based on each vendor's publicly available information as of July 2026. Confirm current features directly with the vendor before deciding.

What Samadhan Rice Mill ERP is

Samadhan Rice Mill ERP (by RITC Pvt Ltd) is built on Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central. Its site highlights automated daily paddy price entry with ancillary-item rates, quality-based pricing that adjusts purchase orders from test results, contract-labour task management with wage calculation, multi-plant management with consolidated inventory, role-tailored dashboards, gate entry with digital weighbridge integration, QC (moisture, Pillai content, mix testing), and loading/dispatch and packing management. Being Dynamics-based, it sits at the enterprise end of the market.

What Millingo is

Millingo, by Svasamm Research, is a purpose-built rice-mill ERP centred on the government Custom Milled Rice (CMR) cycle, per-batch milling recovery, and by-product (bran/husk/broken) accounting, with paddy QC/butta deductions, gunny-bag accounting, and state-specific CMR workflow (West Bengal, Uttar Pradesh, Odisha, Bihar). It's delivered in Starter, Professional, and Enterprise tiers so smaller mills aren't forced onto an enterprise platform.

Which should you choose?

If you specifically want a Microsoft Dynamics 365 platform, strong contract-labour management, and are comfortable at the enterprise tier, Samadhan is worth evaluating. If you want CMR-first software with per-batch recovery and by-product profitability, and a tier that fits a smaller or seasonal mill, Millingo is built for that.
