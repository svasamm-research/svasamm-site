# Directory & Listing Plan (off-page presence)

> Where to list Svasamm and its products to build **backlinks + trust + leads + AI/GEO
> corroboration** — the off-page half of the growth plan (see `growth-plan.md` Part 6).
> These are the "other sites vouch for you" signals a new domain needs to rank.
>
> **One rule above all: consistent NAP** — the same Name, Address, Phone on every listing,
> matching the website footer exactly. And **genuine reviews only** — never fabricate.

## Why bother
Each listing does up to four jobs at once: a **backlink** (authority), a **citation**
(local SEO), a **lead source** (buyers browse these), and a **third-party mention** that
AI engines (ChatGPT/Perplexity/AI Overviews) treat as corroboration. High ROI for the
effort.

## Tier 1 — do first (free, highest ROI)
| Portal | What it gives | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Google Business Profile** | Local map pack, reviews, huge trust | Free | The single biggest local win. One for Svasamm (Konnagar). Consider a separate one for Lucoze if it has its own office/brand presence. |
| **SoftwareSuggest** | India SaaS marketplace, leads + backlink | Free listing | List Millingo, Lucoze, DMS separately. India buyers actively compare here. |
| **Techjockey** | India software marketplace, buyer traffic | Free basic | Same — one listing per product. |
| **GoodFirms** | Reviews + company/product profile + backlink | Free | Complete the profile 100%. |
| **Crunchbase** | Company entity profile (brand/authority) | Free | Helps Google/AI understand "Svasamm Research" as an entity. |
| **LinkedIn Company Page** | Brand, updates, showcase pages per product | Free | Post case studies + guides here; drives referral + trust. |
| **Frappe / ERPNext ecosystem** | Niche audience + backlinks | Free | You build on Frappe — list on the Frappe Marketplace / be active in the ERPNext community. Highly relevant, low competition. |

## Tier 2 — global software review sites (higher authority; reviews are the currency)
| Portal | What it gives | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **G2** | The highest-authority software review site | Free to list | Ranking there is driven by real reviews — ask happy customers. |
| **Capterra / GetApp / Software Advice** | Gartner Digital Markets network, big buyer traffic | Free listing; pay-per-lead optional | One listing propagates across all three. |
| **TrustRadius**, **SourceForge** | Additional review authority + backlinks | Free | Lower priority; do after G2/Capterra. |

## Tier 3 — India B2B + local directories (citations + leads)
| Portal | What it gives | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **IndiaMART**, **TradeIndia** | B2B marketplace; rice-mill & clinic buyers search here | Free + paid | Good for Millingo (agri/industrial buyers). |
| **Justdial**, **Sulekha** | Local + services listings/citations | Free + paid | NAP consistency; local trust. |
| **Bing Places**, **Apple Business Connect** | Map coverage beyond Google | Free | Cheap extra local reach. |

## Tier 4 — niche / industry (compounding, worth the effort)
- **Rice / agri industry portals & news** (e.g. ssricenews, ricenewstoday) — pitch a guest
  article or get a mention; high topical relevance for Millingo.
- **Health-tech / HMS directories** — for Lucoze (also its G2/Capterra "Hospital
  Management" category); ABDM/health ecosystem listings.
- **Product Hunt** — for a launch moment (Lucoze/Millingo), drives a spike + backlinks.
- **Industry associations / chambers** (rice millers' associations, local chambers) —
  membership often includes a directory backlink + real-world credibility.

## Per-product split (list where the buyers actually are)
- **Millingo** → SoftwareSuggest, Techjockey, GoodFirms, IndiaMART, G2/Capterra (ERP
  category), rice/agri portals, Frappe marketplace.
- **Lucoze** → G2/Capterra (Hospital/Clinic Management), SoftwareSuggest, health-tech
  directories, ABDM ecosystem. (Lucoze listings point to **lucoze.com**, not svasamm.com.)
- **DMS** → SoftwareSuggest, Capterra (Distribution/DMS), B2B/manufacturing directories.

## Sequencing (2–3 per week is plenty)
1. Google Business Profile (Svasamm).
2. SoftwareSuggest + Techjockey + GoodFirms (all products).
3. Crunchbase + LinkedIn company page.
4. G2 + Capterra, then start collecting real reviews.
5. IndiaMART/Justdial/Sulekha + Bing/Apple maps.
6. Frappe ecosystem + one niche/industry mention.

## Track it
Watch **referring domains** in Google Search Console (Links report) and Bing Webmaster.
A steady climb = the off-page engine is working. Reviews on G2/Google also feed the local
pack and AI citations.

## Guardrails
- Same NAP everywhere; complete every profile fully (logo, description, categories).
- **Real reviews only** — solicited from actual customers, never invented.
- Don't buy links or use link farms — these directories are legitimate; PBNs are not.
